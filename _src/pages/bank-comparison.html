Beyond the headline

Five things that matter as much as the rate

01

Your spread, not the benchmark

Floating rates are the repo rate plus a spread fixed for you at sanction. The benchmark is the same for everyone; the spread is what you actually negotiate, and it is what makes an old loan drift above the market.

02

Whether the lender funds your property

The cheapest lender is worthless if it will not fund a gram panchayat property, an unapproved deviation or a resale in an older building. Approved-project lists differ sharply between banks.

03

How the lender treats your income

Variable pay, rental income, cash salary and a second business are counted very differently. The same file can be worth ₹10–15 lakh more at one lender than another for this reason alone.

04

The total cost of fees

Processing fee, legal and technical charges, MODT stamp duty, documentation and any bundled insurance. On a ₹50 lakh loan the spread between a lean and a loaded fee structure is easily ₹40,000.

05

Service after disbursement

You will live with this lender for 20 years. How quickly they issue a provisional interest certificate, process a prepayment, or reset your spread on request matters far more than 5 basis points at sanction.

06

The insurance push

A rate quoted “subject to” a single-premium insurance policy funded into the loan is not really that rate. Ask for the rate without the policy and compare that number.
