Make the first meeting count

What to bring, or send ahead on WhatsApp

None of this is mandatory for a first conversation — but with these in hand we can give you a real number instead of a range.

If you are buying

  • Last 3 salary slips, or last 2 years’ ITR if self-employed
  • 6 months’ bank statement
  • PAN and Aadhaar
  • Details of the property, if you have shortlisted one
  • List of your current EMIs

If you are transferring a loan

  • Existing sanction letter
  • Latest loan statement showing the outstanding balance and rate
  • Repayment schedule
  • PAN and Aadhaar

If you are borrowing against property

  • Registered sale deed
  • Latest property tax receipt
  • Approved plan, if you have it
  • Income documents as above
  • Details of any existing mortgage on the property

How we will and will not contact you

We will call or message you from our published number about the enquiry you raised. We will never ask for an OTP, a net-banking password, a card PIN, or a payment to a personal account in exchange for a loan approval.
