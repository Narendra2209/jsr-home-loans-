Read the fine print

What a transfer actually costs

A lower rate is only half the equation. These are the charges that decide whether the switch is worth it — ask for every one of them in writing before you sign.

Processing fee (new lender)
0.25%–1% of the loan, often capped, and frequently waived during campaigns. Always ask.
Legal & technical valuation
₹3,000–₹10,000. The new lender re-verifies the title and re-values the property.
Stamp duty on fresh mortgage (MODT)
Charged by the state on the new mortgage deed. Budget for this — it is often the largest single line.
Foreclosure charge (old lender)
Nil on floating-rate home loans to individuals. Fixed-rate loans may attract 2%–4% of the outstanding.
Document retrieval / handling
₹500–₹5,000 to release your original property papers.
Insurance bundling
Not compulsory. If a lender insists on a policy to release the rate, count its premium as part of the cost of the loan.

The rule of thumb

Transfer if the rate gap is 0.4% or more, more than 7 years of tenure remain, and the calculator above shows you breaking even within 18 months. Below that, it is usually cheaper to ask your existing lender to reset your spread instead — which they will often do for a small conversion fee.
