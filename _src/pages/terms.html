We are not a bank, an NBFC, or a lender registered with the Reserve Bank of India. We do not lend money, do not accept deposits, and have no authority to sanction, approve, reject or price any loan. Every credit decision belongs solely to the lender, under its own policy, and the loan agreement is between you and that lender.

2. Our fees

Our advisory and application support is provided free of charge to you. We are remunerated by the lender in the form of a commission when a loan we have sourced is disbursed. This means:

  • You do not pay us for a sanction, an approval, or a “file processing” charge.
  • Any charge you do pay — processing fee, legal and technical valuation, stamp duty, MODT, insurance premium — is levied by the lender or a statutory authority, is disclosed to you before you pay it, and is paid directly to that party, never in cash to an individual.
  • Because a lender pays us, you should read our recommendations knowing that commission structures vary between lenders. We will tell you plainly if the lender we are recommending is not the cheapest and why we still think it is right for your case.

If anyone asks you to pay a fee in cash, to a personal bank account or to a UPI ID in our name in exchange for a loan approval, it is not us. Please report it to us immediately.

  • A timeline. Estimated timelines depend on the lender, the valuer, the advocate, the property and how complete your documents are.
  • The lender’s conduct. We are not responsible for a lender’s decisions, service levels, rate changes, charges or the terms of the loan agreement you sign with it.

5. Your responsibilities

  • Give us accurate, complete and genuine information and documents. Submitting forged or altered documents is a criminal offence, and we will withdraw from any file where we suspect it.
  • Tell us about all existing loans, credit cards and obligations. Undisclosed liabilities are found at credit appraisal and cause rejections.
  • Read the sanction letter, loan agreement and Key Facts Statement yourself before signing. Ask us about anything you do not understand — that is what we are for.
  • Make your own independent checks on the property, including its title, approvals and the developer. The lender’s legal and technical appraisal is for the lender’s benefit, not a warranty to you.
