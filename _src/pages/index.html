How it works

From first call to disbursement in 6 steps

You deal with one point of contact throughout. We handle the follow-ups with the bank.

  1. 1

    Free consultation

    Tell us your income, the property and your budget. We tell you honestly what is achievable — on the phone, on WhatsApp or at our LB Nagar office.

    Day 0
  2. 2

    Eligibility check & lender shortlist

    We run your numbers, pull your credit position and shortlist the two or three lenders whose policy actually fits your profile.

    Day 1
  3. 3

    Document collection

    A single checklist, collected once. We check every page before it goes in so the file is not returned for a missing signature.

    Day 1–3
  4. 4

    Application login

    Your file is logged in with the chosen lender, the processing fee is paid and credit appraisal begins.

    Day 3–4
  5. 5

    Legal, technical & credit appraisal

    The lender values the property and verifies the title chain. We chase the valuer, the advocate and the credit manager so nothing sits idle.

    Day 4–8
  6. 6

    Sanction & disbursement

    We walk you through the sanction letter line by line before you sign, then coordinate disbursement to the builder or seller.

    Day 7–15

Timelines are typical for a complete salaried file. Self-employed, NRI and resale property cases can take longer, mainly because of property documentation.
