About JSR Home Loan Services

We work for you, not for the bank

JSR Home Loan Services is the loan facilitation arm of JSR Financial Associates, based in LB Nagar, Hyderabad. We started with a simple observation: most people do not lose a home loan because they cannot afford it. They lose it because the file went to the wrong lender, a document was missing, or nobody explained what the sanction letter actually said.

So we built the practice around the opposite of that. One point of contact. One document checklist. A shortlist of lenders chosen because their policy fits your profile — salaried, self-employed or NRI — and a straight answer when a case is not fundable yet.

Since then we have advised more than 2,000 families and businesses across Hyderabad on home purchase, construction, balance transfer, loan against property and business finance.

Founder’s note

Why we started JSR

“I spent years watching good applications get rejected for bad reasons — a file sent to a lender that never funds that category of property, an income document nobody asked for until week three, a rate quoted verbally and never honoured in writing. Families were being asked to navigate a system nobody had explained to them.

JSR exists to sit on the customer’s side of that table. We tell you what you can actually borrow before you book a flat, we show you the full cost of the loan and not just the headline rate, and we stay on the file until the money reaches the seller.”

Important disclosure

JSR Home Loan Services is the loan facilitation arm of JSR Financial Associates. We work as a loan facilitator / Direct Selling Agent (DSA) for banks and housing finance companies. We are not a bank, an NBFC or a lender registered with the Reserve Bank of India. We do not lend money and we do not accept deposits.

Every application is assessed, sanctioned and disbursed solely by the partner bank or housing finance company, at its sole discretion and subject to its own credit policy; the loan agreement is between you and that lender. Interest rates, processing fees, eligibility figures and timelines shown on this website are indicative only, are taken from lender disclosures, change without notice, and vary with your credit profile, income and property. Calculator results are estimates for planning purposes and are not a sanction, an offer, or financial, tax or legal advice. Always confirm the final terms in the sanction letter issued by the lender before you commit.

Stay safe: we never ask for a fee in cash or to a personal account in exchange for a loan approval, and we will never ask for your OTP, net-banking password or card PIN.
