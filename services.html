What Service We Provide

Construction Loan

Building a new home requires timely funds. Our Construction Loans are designed to provide you with easy financing for house construction, offering quick disbursements in stages as your project progresses. With competitive interest rates and flexible repayment options, we ensure your dream home is built without financial stress.

Home Buying Loan

Buying a house is one of life’s biggest milestones. Our Home Buying Loans provide you with the financial support you need to purchase your dream property. With low interest rates, fast approval, and minimal documentation, we make your home buying journey smooth and hassle-free.

Home Loan Balance Transfer

Why pay more when you can save? With our Home Loan Balance Transfer service, you can easily switch your existing home loan to JSR Home Loan Services and enjoy lower EMIs, reduced interest rates, and better repayment flexibility. We help you maximise your savings while enjoying improved loan terms.

Home Renovation Loan

Give your home a fresh look with our Home Renovation Loan. Whether it’s a complete makeover or small upgrades, we provide quick and affordable financing for home improvement projects. From interiors to extensions, our renovation loans help you increase the comfort and value of your home.

Home Mortgage Loan

Unlock the value of your property with our Home Mortgage Loan. By pledging your property, you can access funds for personal or business needs at affordable interest rates. With flexible repayment options, and fast approvals, our mortgage loans give you financial freedom while keeping your property safe.

Commercial Property Loan

Our Commercial Property Loans provide affordable financing for purchasing or constructing office spaces, shops, warehouses, and other business premises. With flexible repayment terms, competitive interest rates, and quick approvals, we make property investments stress-free and profitable.

Open Plots Purchase Loan

Our Open Plots Purchase Loans are designed to help you buy land for future construction or investment. With low-interest rates, easy EMIs, and hassle-free processing, we make land ownership simple and achievable.

Personal Loan

Our Personal Loans give you the freedom to handle expenses like education, medical needs, travel, weddings, or emergencies. With quick disbursal, no collateral, and flexible tenure, we ensure you get funds when you need them most.

Business Loan

Our Business Loans are tailored to support entrepreneurs, SMEs, and established businesses. Whether for working capital, expansion, equipment purchase, or cash flow management, we offer financing that fuels success.
