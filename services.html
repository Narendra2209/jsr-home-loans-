JSR Home Loan Services

What we arrange

Nine loan types, one point of contact, and the same approach to all of them: work out what is realistically achievable, place the file with the lender most likely to approve it well, and stay on it until the money lands.

Construction Loan

Building on a plot you already own needs money released in step with the work, not all at once. A construction loan disburses in stages against your engineer’s certificate as the slab, brickwork and finishing progress, and you pay interest only on what has been drawn. Approved plan and clear title on the land are the two things to get right before you apply.

Home Buying Loan

For a flat, villa or independent house — new, under construction or resale. Up to 90% funding, tenure up to 30 years, and rates that start lower than any other loan you can take. Full eligibility, documents and rates →

Home Loan Balance Transfer

If your loan is more than two or three years old, you are probably paying above the rate a new borrower gets today. We compare, negotiate with your existing lender first, and move the loan only if the numbers justify it. Savings calculator →

Home Renovation Loan

Interiors, an extra floor, a kitchen rebuild or overdue repairs. A renovation loan is secured against the house, so it is priced far below a personal loan — typically the cheapest way to fund work on a property you already own. Quotes and estimates from your contractor are usually needed.

Home Mortgage Loan

Raise funds against a property you already own, for business, education, medical costs or consolidating expensive debt. You keep possession throughout. Property eligibility, LTV and documents →

Commercial Property Loan

Financing to buy or build office space, a shop, a showroom or a warehouse. Funding is generally a notch lower than for residential property and the valuation matters more, so location and marketability drive the outcome. Lease income from the property can often support eligibility.

Open Plots Purchase Loan

For land in an approved layout — HMDA, DTCP or equivalent. Plot loans fund a lower share of value than home loans and run shorter tenures, and unapproved layouts are declined outright. Send us the layout approval before you pay an advance; it is the single most common reason a plot file fails.

Personal Loan

Unsecured funding for education, a medical emergency, a wedding or travel. No collateral, quick disbursal, tenure up to 5–7 years. Priced much higher than a secured loan, so if you own property it is worth checking a loan against property first.

Business Loan

Working capital, expansion, equipment or cash-flow smoothing for proprietors, SMEs and established firms. Both unsecured business loans and secured facilities against property, depending on the ticket size and what the numbers in your financials support.
