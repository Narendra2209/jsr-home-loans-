JSR Home Loan Services, the loan facilitation arm of JSR Financial Associates (“we”, “us”), takes the personal and financial information you share with us seriously. This policy explains what we collect, why, who we pass it to, and what you can ask us to do about it.

1. Who we are

JSR Financial Associates, 11-14-518/1, 103, Amrutha Pride, Siri Nagar Colony, LB Nagar, Hyderabad – 500074, Telangana, India. We act as a loan facilitator and Direct Selling Agent for banks and housing finance companies. We are not a lender.

For any question about this policy or your information, contact us at jsrhomeloans@gmail.com or +91 90007 81967.

2. What we collect

Information you give us directly — when you fill in a form on this website, message us on WhatsApp, email us or walk into our office:

  • Name, mobile number, email address and postal address
  • Employment type, income details and existing loan obligations
  • Loan requirement, property details and location
  • Where you proceed with an application: KYC documents (PAN, Aadhaar, passport), income documents (salary slips, Form 16, ITR, bank statements, financial statements), property documents and existing loan statements

Information collected automatically when you browse this site: IP address, browser and device type, pages viewed and referring page. This is standard web-server and analytics data.

What we do not collect. We never ask for and you should never give us your net-banking password, card PIN, CVV or any OTP. Anyone asking you for these in our name is not us.

The calculators on this site — the EMI calculator, eligibility checker and balance transfer calculator — run entirely in your browser. The figures you enter are not sent to us unless you choose to share them through the WhatsApp or email button.

3. Why we use it

  • To respond to your enquiry and give you advice on loan options
  • To assess your indicative eligibility and shortlist suitable lenders
  • To prepare and submit your loan application to the lender or lenders you choose
  • To follow up on the status of your application and coordinate valuation, legal and disbursement steps
  • To keep records that we are required to retain as a facilitator, and to comply with law
  • To send you service updates about your live application

We will not use your information for marketing unrelated products, and we do not sell it to anyone.

4. Who we share it with

  • Banks and housing finance companies you have asked us to approach. This is the core of what we do — an application cannot be made without sharing your documents with the lender. We share it only with the lenders you have agreed to.
  • Service providers acting for the lender: valuers, empanelled advocates, verification agencies.
  • Credit information companies, where the lender pulls your credit report as part of the application. That pull is made by the lender under its own authority and consent.
  • Government or regulatory authorities, where we are required by law to disclose.

We do not share your information with anyone else, and we do not sell or rent contact lists.

5. Consent, and taking it back

We process your information on the basis of the consent you give us when you submit an enquiry or hand over documents. You can withdraw that consent at any time by writing to jsrhomeloans@gmail.com.

Withdrawing consent stops us using your information going forward. It cannot undo an application already submitted to a lender — at that point the lender holds your data under its own policy and you would need to approach the lender directly — and it does not affect records we are required by law to retain.

6. Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • Tell you what personal information about you we hold and who we have shared it with
  • Correct anything that is wrong, incomplete or out of date
  • Erase information we no longer need for the purpose you gave it for, subject to legal retention requirements
  • Stop contacting you

Write to jsrhomeloans@gmail.com with the words “Data request” in the subject line. We will respond within [30] days. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may escalate to the relevant authority under applicable Indian data protection law.

7. How long we keep it

Enquiries that do not proceed to an application: [12 months] from your last contact with us. Completed applications: [8 years] from disbursement or rejection, in line with the record-keeping we are expected to maintain as a facilitator and for tax purposes. After that, records are securely destroyed.

8. How we protect it

Physical documents are held in locked storage at our office and only staff working on your file have access. Electronic records are held on password-protected devices and accounts. Documents shared over WhatsApp or email are subject to the security of those platforms, which we do not control.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. If a breach affecting your information occurs, we will inform you and the relevant authority as required by law.

9. Cookies and analytics

This website uses only what is needed to make it work, plus [analytics, if you add it — name the tool]. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts and the contact page embeds a Google Map, so Google receives your IP address when those load. You can block cookies in your browser; the site will still function.

10. Third-party links

This site links to lender websites, Google Maps, WhatsApp and other third parties. Their privacy practices are their own and this policy does not cover them.

11. Children

Our services are for adults. We do not knowingly collect information about anyone under 18. If you believe we have, tell us and we will delete it.

12. Changes

We may update this policy. The current version is always on this page with the date at the top. Material changes affecting how we use information you have already given us will be notified to you directly.

If someone contacts you claiming to be us

We will never ask for an OTP, a password, a card PIN, or a payment to a personal account or UPI ID in exchange for a loan approval. If you receive such a request in our name, do not act on it — call us on +91 90007 81967.

Important disclosure

JSR Home Loan Services is the loan facilitation arm of JSR Financial Associates. We work as a loan facilitator / Direct Selling Agent (DSA) for banks and housing finance companies. We are not a bank, an NBFC or a lender registered with the Reserve Bank of India. We do not lend money and we do not accept deposits.

Every application is assessed, sanctioned and disbursed solely by the partner bank or housing finance company, at its sole discretion and subject to its own credit policy; the loan agreement is between you and that lender. Interest rates, processing fees, eligibility figures and timelines shown on this website are indicative only, are taken from lender disclosures, change without notice, and vary with your credit profile, income and property. Calculator results are estimates for planning purposes and are not a sanction, an offer, or financial, tax or legal advice. Always confirm the final terms in the sanction letter issued by the lender before you commit.

Stay safe: we never ask for a fee in cash or to a personal account in exchange for a loan approval, and we will never ask for your OTP, net-banking password or card PIN.
