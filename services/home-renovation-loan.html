Improving what you already own

Repair, extend or redo the house you already have.

A renovation loan funds work on a property you own — rewiring, a new kitchen, an extra room, waterproofing, a full interior redo. It is secured against the same property, which is why it costs far less than a personal loan for the same work.

Why through JSR

What you get

  • Much cheaper than a personal loan

    Because your property secures it, the rate is roughly half what an unsecured loan for the same work would cost.

  • Structural or cosmetic

    Extensions, plumbing and rewiring, waterproofing, flooring, a new kitchen, painting — most lenders fund all of it.

  • Top-up on an existing loan

    If you already have a home loan with a good record, a top-up is often faster and cheaper than a fresh renovation loan.

  • Tax relief on the interest

    Up to ₹30,000 a year is deductible on a self-occupied property — inside the overall ₹2 lakh limit.

Is this you?

Who this suits

  • You own the property outright or have a home loan on it in good standing
  • The work is on an existing structure, not new construction on empty land
  • You have a written estimate from a contractor
  • Structural changes have local authority approval where one is required

Ask about a top-up before you apply for anything new

If you already have a home loan and have paid it well for a couple of years, your existing lender can often add a top-up at close to your current rate, with almost no fresh paperwork. It is usually cheaper and always faster than a new renovation loan. We will check that first — even though it earns us less.
