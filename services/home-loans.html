The process

How your home loan gets done

  1. 1

    Eligibility and document review

    We check what you qualify for and tell you honestly where you stand before you apply.

  2. 2

    Application to the right lenders

    We place your file with the banks most likely to say yes, and follow it up daily.

  3. 3

    Sanction, valuation and legal clearance

    The bank values the property and checks the title. We coordinate both and chase the queries.

  4. 4

    Disbursement

    Funds are released to the builder or seller. We stay on it until the money moves.
